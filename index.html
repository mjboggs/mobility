ALD Safety Reminders

  • ✅ Use a wall, counter, chair, or cane for support.
  • ✅ Stop if balance feels unsafe.
  • ✅ If foot dragging increases, reduce fatigue and focus on the Foot Booster routine.
  • ✅ Quality of movement is more important than completing every repetition.
  • ✅ If fatigue is high, complete only the Foot Booster section for the day.
  • ✅ Fall prevention is the highest priority.

Edema Safety Reminders

  • ✅ Review this routine with your vascular doctor, and follow their instructions on compression and blood thinners.
  • ✅ Wear your compression stockings as prescribed. Put them on in the morning before swelling builds.
  • ✅ Moves should feel easy and pain-free. Stop if anything hurts.
  • ✅ Skip deep leg massage unless your doctor says it's OK (stents and clot history).
  • ✅ Use a chair or counter for support when standing.
🚨 Call your doctor right away or go to the ER for sudden new or worse swelling in one leg, calf pain or tenderness, warmth or redness, or skin that splits or weeps.
Call 911 for chest pain or sudden shortness of breath.

      Daily Swelling Habits

      • 🧦 Compression stockings on first thing in the morning
      • 🛋️ Feet above heart level for 15–20 min, 3–4 times a day
      • ⏱️ Move every 30–60 min (do a Sitting Break)
      • 🚶 Walk every day. Build up to 20–30 min.
      • 🧂 Go easy on salt. Drink water as your doctor advises.
      • 🚫 Don't cross your legs. Avoid tight sock tops.
      • 👣 Check feet and toes daily for skin breaks. Moisturize, but not between toes.
